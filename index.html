About Our Restaurant

Welcome to Annapoorneshwari Andhra Mess

❖

At Annapoorneshwari Andhra Mess, we celebrate the authentic flavours of Andhra cuisine. Our goal is to offer customers a satisfying food experience inspired by traditional South Indian meals, familiar flavours, and the warmth of home-style cooking.

Visit us to enjoy a delicious meal in a welcoming atmosphere. Whether you are craving the rich aroma of ghee and gun powder podi with hot rice, or hearty traditional accompaniments, we are delighted to serve you.

Home-Style Warmth

Comforting, wholesome food served with genuine hospitality.

Authentic Andhra Spices

Traditional recipes rich in native Andhra aromatics and taste.
